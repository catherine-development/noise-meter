{# CSRF plumbing, included from the of every page. The token is in a meta tag rather than inlined at each call site, and fetch is wrapped once here rather than edited in forty places, so a fetch() added anywhere in the app later is protected without anyone remembering to do it. HTML forms still carry their own hidden csrf_token field — a form posts without touching JavaScript. Only same-origin, state-changing requests get the header: an outbound call to Open-Meteo or anywhere else must not leak the token. #}
